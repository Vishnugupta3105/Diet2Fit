Frequently Asked Questions

Can I follow an Indian diet and still lose weight?

add
Absolutely. A well-planned Indian diet with the right balance of dal, roti, rice, sabzi, and curd can be highly effective for weight loss. Dt. Disha creates plans using familiar Indian foods that fit your daily routine and food culture.

Do I need to stop eating rice to lose weight?

add
No. Rice is not the enemy. The key is portion control, meal timing, and balancing rice with adequate protein, fiber, and healthy fats. Dt. Disha helps you include rice in a structured way that supports your weight loss goals.

How much weight can I expect to lose per month?

add
Healthy, sustainable weight loss is typically 2-4 kg per month. This varies based on your starting weight, metabolism, activity level, and adherence. Dt. Disha focuses on fat loss rather than just scale weight, ensuring long-term results.

What makes this different from a diet app?

add
Unlike generic plans, Dt. Disha personally reviews your lab reports, medical history, lifestyle, stress levels, and food preferences before creating your plan. You get 1-on-1 video consultations, regular plan revisions, and direct WhatsApp support — not an algorithm.

Is the consultation done online?

add
Yes, all consultations are conducted online via video call. You can consult from anywhere in India. Your personalized diet plan is shared digitally, and ongoing support happens via WhatsApp.
