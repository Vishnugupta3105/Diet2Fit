Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Beyond Kilos ("we", "our", or "us"), led by Clinical Dietitian Dt. Disha (M.Sc. Clinical Nutrition), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and medical/health information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you visit gobeyondkilos.com or use our consultation services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, schedule a consultation, or purchase a dietary plan, we may collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, age, gender, and country of residence.
  • Health & Wellness Data: Current weight, height, BMI, dietary preferences, lifestyle details, medical history (e.g., PCOS, thyroid, diabetes, cardiac conditions), and lab reports you choose to share for personalized meal planning.
  • Transaction Details: Plan selection, payment date, transaction reference IDs, and amount. We do not store full credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve our clinical nutrition services, including:

  • Developing tailored, science-backed dietary and meal plans.
  • Scheduling one-on-one nutrition consultations via video or phone.
  • Providing continuous WhatsApp coaching, progress tracking, and portal updates.
  • Processing plan subscription payments and issuing receipts.
  • Communicating important service updates and health recommendations.

3. Payment Processing & Security

All online payments on Beyond Kilos are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (including Razorpay). When you enter payment information, it is securely transmitted directly to the gateway using bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store your full payment card number, CVV, or banking passwords.

4. Data Confidentiality & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health data to third-party marketers or advertisers. Your data is strictly shared only with:

  • Dt. Disha and the authorized Beyond Kilos clinical advisory team directly responsible for your dietary management.
  • Trusted technological service providers (such as encrypted hosting, database, and SMS/WhatsApp notification APIs) strictly necessary to deliver our services.
  • Regulatory or legal authorities if required by applicable Indian or international law.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You have the right to access, review, correct, or request deletion of your personal records and consultation history at any time. Simply email us at dieticiandisha29@gmail.com.
