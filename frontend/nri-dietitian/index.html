Why Eating Healthy Abroad Is Challenging for Indians

Moving overseas often leads to unintended weight gain, rising HbA1c, elevated cholesterol, or worsening PCOS symptoms. Here is why standard Western diet advice fails Indian bodies:

🥗

Western Diets Ignore Desi Food

Local dietitians abroad often prescribe cold salads, kale bowls, or unfamiliar meals that are unsustainable long-term. You don't need to quit roti, daal, or curry to be fit.

❄️

Cold Weather & Desk Jobs

Long desk hours in North America and Europe, harsh winters with minimal sunlight (Vitamin D deficiency), and car-centric lifestyles reduce daily energy expenditure significantly.

🛒

Supermarket Hidden Traps

Packaged foods in US/UK/Canada often contain hidden sugars, refined seed oils, and high sodium. We teach you how to read nutrition labels and pick the healthiest local staples.
