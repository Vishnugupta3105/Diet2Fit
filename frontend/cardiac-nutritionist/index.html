Nutrition for Heart Health

What you eat directly impacts your cholesterol levels, blood pressure, inflammation, and arterial health. A well-designed cardiac nutrition plan doesn't mean eating bland, boring food — it means making strategic choices that protect your heart while still enjoying delicious Indian meals.

Cholesterol Management

Foods rich in soluble fiber, omega-3s, and plant sterols can lower LDL while supporting HDL — your heart's natural protection.

Blood Pressure Support

Strategic sodium reduction, potassium-rich foods, and the DASH-inspired approach adapted for Indian cuisine.

Anti-Inflammatory Diet

Chronic inflammation damages blood vessels. Anti-inflammatory foods like turmeric, garlic, nuts, and fatty fish help protect your arteries.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a cardiac nutritionist do?

add
A cardiac nutritionist specializes in diet plans that support heart health — managing cholesterol, blood pressure, inflammation, and weight. Dt. Disha interprets your lipid profile and creates a plan using heart-healthy Indian foods.

Can Indian food be heart-healthy?

add
Absolutely. Many Indian foods are excellent for heart health — dal, whole grains, vegetables, nuts, seeds, fish, and anti-inflammatory spices. The key is reducing saturated fats, excess salt, and refined carbohydrates while increasing fiber, omega-3s, and antioxidants.

Can nutrition lower cholesterol without medication?

add
Dietary changes can meaningfully reduce cholesterol levels. However, whether medication is needed depends on your overall cardiovascular risk. Nutrition therapy works alongside medical treatment, not as a replacement.

Is this suitable after a heart surgery?

add
Yes, with coordination with your cardiologist. Post-cardiac event nutrition is critical for recovery and prevention. Dt. Disha can create a cardiac rehabilitation nutrition plan aligned with your cardiologist's recommendations.
