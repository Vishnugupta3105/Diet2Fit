Plans Built for Your Regional Indian Kitchen

Is an online dietitian as effective as in-person?

add
Yes. Clinical studies show online nutrition counseling is as effective as in-person visits, with the added advantage of regular WhatsApp check-ins, lab report review, and flexible scheduling from home.
