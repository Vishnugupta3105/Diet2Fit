What Your HbA1c Report Means & How to Support Lower Numbers With Indian Food

By Dt. Disha • M.Sc. Specialized Dietetics (Diabetes & Cardiac Nutrition), SVT College • Clinically Reviewed: Nov 25, 2026
Quick HbA1c Summary

HbA1c measures your 2-to-3-month average blood sugar level. Normal is below 5.7%, prediabetes is 5.7% to 6.4%, and diabetes is 6.5% or above. Alongside your physician's care, Indian meals support healthier HbA1c readings when you eat vegetables/fiber first, include 20–30g of protein at every meal, control refined carbohydrate portions, and walk for 10–15 minutes after meals.

HbA1c Reference Chart

HbA1c Result Clinical Category Estimated Avg. Glucose (eAG)
Below 5.7% Normal range Under ~117 mg/dL (6.5 mmol/L in Canada)
5.7% – 6.4% Prediabetes ~117 – 137 mg/dL (6.5 – 7.6 mmol/L)
6.5% or higher Diabetes range (confirm with physician) 140 mg/dL+ (7.8+ mmol/L)

Why Can Fasting Sugar Be Normal When HbA1c Is High?

Many Indian clients — especially South Asians in India, the US, and Canada — are surprised when their morning fasting glucose is 95 mg/dL yet their HbA1c comes back at 6.1% or 6.8%.

That happens because HbA1c captures all your post-meal spikes across 24 hours. Traditional Indian breakfasts (plain poha, upma, idli-chutney, or aloo paratha with sweet chai) are mostly fast-digesting carbohydrates with very little protein. Blood sugar surges 1–2 hours after breakfast and lunch, pushing your 3-month HbA1c upward even if your overnight fasting level looks normal.

5 Practical Indian Diet Shifts to Support Better HbA1c

  1. Upgrade Breakfast From Carb-Heavy to Protein-Anchored: Swap plain poha or toast for moong dal chilla with paneer, 2–3 eggs with sautéed vegetables, or Greek yogurt with chia/flax seeds and nuts.
  2. Use the 50-25-25 Indian Thali: Fill 50% of your plate with non-starchy sabzi & salad, 25% with thick dal/chana/paneer/tofu/eggs/fish, and 25% with jowar/bajra/besan-wheat roti or portioned brown/aged rice.
  3. Follow Fiber-First Sequencing: Eat your cucumber-tomato salad or cooked greens before your roti or rice to reduce the post-meal glucose spike.
  4. Watch Liquid Calories & Tea-Time Biscuits: 2–3 cups of full-sugar chai with Marie/digestive biscuits daily can quietly add 40–50g of refined sugar and flour.
  5. Take a 10–15 Minute Walk After Meals: Light movement after lunch and dinner helps your muscles absorb circulating glucose without requiring extra insulin.

Medical Note: Nutrition therapy works alongside your doctor's prescribed treatment, never as a replacement. Always share lab changes with your physician before adjusting any diabetes medication.
